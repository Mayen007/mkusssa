Privacy Policy

We collect only the information needed to manage membership requests, communicate with you, and support the work of MKUSSSA.

Last updated: May 28, 2026

This page explains how Mount Kenya University South Sudanese Students' Association (MKUSSSA) handles personal information submitted through the website.

Information we collect

When you submit the membership form or contact us through the website, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, registration number, course, and the message you send.

How we use your information

We use this information to review membership requests, respond to inquiries, maintain internal records, and communicate with members and applicants about association activities.

How we store and protect data

Submitted information is stored in our administrative systems and is accessible only to authorized association administrators. We take reasonable steps to protect it from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Sharing information

We do not sell personal information. We may share data only when required to operate the association, comply with the law, or respond to a valid request from university or legal authorities.

Retention

We keep submitted data for as long as it is needed for membership administration, record keeping, and association operations, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Your choices

If you would like us to update or remove information you submitted, contact the association using the details below.
